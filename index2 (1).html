About Stories & Lessons

This website features different books and stories that share meaningful ideas about life, friendship, dreams, personal growth, and the importance of making good choices.

Each story offers something different to learn and gives readers a chance to discover new ideas, perspectives, and lessons that they can apply in everyday life.

Featured Stories

Choose a story and lesson.

The Alchemist

The Alchemist encourages readers to follow their dreams, passion, and destiny. It teaches that trusting yourself and pursuing your dreams can lead to self-discovery and growth.

No Man is an island

No Man Is an Island teaches that people need connection, love, and community to live a meaningful life. It also emphasizes sincerity, mercy, and personal growth.

A Million Miles in a Thousand Years

A Million Miles in a Thousand Years teaches that a meaningful life requires intentional choices, courage, and taking risks instead of simply waiting for things to happen.

Let’s Learn About the Forest: A Seek-and-Find Story Through God’s Creation

A seek-and-find board book where children help Mama Chipmunk explore the forest, discovering woodland creatures while learning about God's creation.

Adventure Time

Adventure Time is not only about adventure. It is also about friendship, teamwork, strategies, and caring for the people around you.

Atomic Habits

Atomic Habits teaches that small, consistent improvements can create major results over time. It focuses on building good habits through simple systems and breaking bad habits by making them less obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying.
